# Documented Military Testimonies: UFOs at Nuclear Facilities (1945-Present)

## Introduction
Military personnel, including pilots, commanders, and nuclear weapons officers, have provided extensive documented testimony regarding UFO incursions at nuclear facilities. These testimonies represent some of the most credible evidence in UFO research, coming from highly trained observers with security clearances.

## Key Cases with Military Testimony

### 1. Malmstrom Air Force Base, Montana (March 16, 1967)
**Location:** Malmstrom AFB, Montana - Minuteman ICBM sites
**Witnesses:** Multiple Air Force officers and security personnel

**Primary Testimony:**
- **Captain Robert Salas (Deputy Missile Combat Crew Commander):**
  - Testified that a UFO was reported hovering over the Oscar Flight Launch Control Center
  - Subsequently, all 10 Minuteman ICBMs under his command went offline (became "no-go")
  - Technical investigation found no mechanical failures
  - Official Air Force response attributed it to "electromagnetic pulse" or "atmospheric conditions"

- **First Lieutenant Robert Jacobs:**
  - Documented testimony regarding UFO activity at missile sites
  - Reported that missiles were rendered inoperable during UFO sightings

**Official Response:**
- Air Force classified the incident as "unknown"
- Documents later released through Freedom of Information Act (FOIA)
- No official explanation provided for UFO sighting or missile failures

### 2. Minot Air Force Base, North Dakota (October 1968)
**Location:** Minot AFB, North Dakota - Minuteman missile complex
**Witnesses:** B-52 crew, Minot tower personnel, missile security teams

**Primary Testimony:**
- **B-52 Radar Navigator:** Reported UFO intercepting their aircraft during training mission
- **Minot Tower Operators:** Documented visual and radar contact with unknown objects
- **Missile Security Teams:** Witnessed UFO hovering over missile sites

**Official Response:**
- Project Blue Book investigated and classified as "unknown"
- Crew members underwent extensive debriefing
- Electronic reconnaissance recordings were confiscated

### 3. Loring Air Force Base, Maine (October 27-28, 1975)
**Location:** Loring AFB, Maine - Strategic nuclear bomber base
**Witnesses:** Base security, B-52 crew members, wing command staff

**Primary Testimony:**
- **Security Sergeant:** Reported disc-shaped craft over nuclear weapons storage area
- **Base Commander Colonel:** Issued official sighting reports
- **Air Traffic Controllers:** Tracked unknown objects on radar approaching weapons storage

**Official Response:**
- NORAD scrambled fighter jets (never made visual contact)
- Base placed on heightened security alert
- All personnel directed to report future sightings immediately

### 4. RAF Bentwaters/Woodbridge, UK (December 1980)
**Location:** RAF Bentwaters and RAF Woodbridge, Suffolk, UK - NATO nuclear storage
**Witnesses:** Multiple USAF personnel including base commander

**Primary Testimony:**
- **Lieutenant Colonel Charles Halt (Deputy Base Commander):**
  - Recorded live audio during UFO encounter incident
  - Witnessed unknown object firing beams of light into weapons storage area
  - Provided extensive oral history testimony to historians

- **Security Personnel:** Multiple witnesses to UFO landing in Rendlesham Forest adjacent to base
- **Radar Operators:** Tracked unknown objects on approach to nuclear storage areas

**Official Response:**
- Initially downplayed as "misidentified lighthouse"
- Subsequent FOIA releases revealed classified "Halt Memo"
- British Ministry of Defence classified records until 2000 release

### 5. Kirtland Air Force Base, New Mexico (November 1975)
**Location:** Kirtland AFB, New Mexico - Sandia nuclear weapons complex
**Witnesses:** Security personnel, Sandia security teams

**Primary Testimony:**
- **Security Officers:** Documented multiple glowing objects over Manzano Nuclear Weapons Storage Area
- **Base Personnel:** Witnessed craft performing "impossible maneuvers" over restricted airspace

**Official Response:**
- AFOSI (Air Force Office of Special Investigations) conducted extensive inquiry
- Personnel subjected to polygraph examinations
- All witnesses required to sign security agreements

### 6. F.E. Warren Air Force Base, Wyoming (January 2011)
**Location:** F.E. Warren AFB, Wyoming - Minuteman III missile wings
**Witnesses:** Missile maintenance teams, security personnel

**Primary Testimony:**
- **Missile Launch Officers:** Reported temporary loss of communication with multiple ICBMs
- **Maintenance Personnel:** Witnessed unusual aerial phenomena during system failures

**Official Response:**
- Air Force initially denied any connections between UFO reports and missile failures
- Later acknowledged "unexplained outages" coincided with pilot UFO sightings in same areas
- FOIA requests yielded limited documentation

### 7. Big Sur, California (September 15, 1964)
**Location:** Vandenberg Air Force Base test range - Nike nuclear missile test
**Witnesses:** Photo-optical instrumentation team, range safety officers

**Primary Testimony:**
- **Dr. Robert Jacobs (Lieutenant, USAF):**
  - Filmed UFO intercepting dummy nuclear warhead during test launch
  - Subsequently debriefed by CIA personnel
  - Ordered to remain silent under security classification
  - Later provided sworn testimony to researchers

**Official Response:**
- Film confiscated by Air Force intelligence
- All personnel required to sign security oaths
- Jacobs threatened with court martial for disclosure

### 8. Vandenberg Launch Complex, California (Multiple dates 1966-1967)
**Location:** Vandenberg AFB - Atlas and Thor missile tests
**Witnesses:** Launch crews, range safety personnel, tracking operators

**Primary Testimony:**
- **Missile Launch Officers:** Consistent reports of UFOs during dummy warhead tests

**Official Response:**

- Personnel sworn to secrecy

## Documented Patterns from Military Testimony

### Consistent Elements Across Testimonies:
1. **Electronic Interference:** UFO sightings often coincided with nuclear weapons system malfunctions
2. **Communication Disruption:** Radio and electronic systems frequently failed during encounters
3. **Weapons Systems Disabling:** Multiple cases where ICBMs were rendered inoperable
4. **Security Protocol Activation:** Bases often placed on high alert following incidents
5. **Document Classification:** Most incidents classified at high levels initially

### Common Official Responses:
1. **Denial:** Public denial while conducting classified investigations
2. **Misidentification:** Often attributed to celestial phenomena or aircraft
3. **Classification:** Security oaths and classification of evidence
4. **No Comment:** Refusal to discuss nuclear weapons security implications
5. **Delayed Acknowledgment:** Acknowledgment only decades later through FOIA releases
