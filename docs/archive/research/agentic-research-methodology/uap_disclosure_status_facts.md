# Disclosure Status Tracking - Verified Facts and Cross-Check Summary

## Current State (as of 2024)

### 1. Government Progression
**Verified Fact**: UAP Disclosure Act removed from National Defense Authorization Act (NDAA) 2024
- **Source**: Congressional record, December 2023
- **Status**: FAILED - Key transparency provisions eliminated

**Cross-Check**: Senator Marco Rubio and other Intelligence Committee members publicly acknowledged this significant setback to disclosure efforts.

### 2. Pentagon AARO Status
**Verified Fact**: All-domain Anomaly Resolution Office (AARO) remains operational as DoD's primary UAP investigation office
- **Established**: July 2022 via NDAA
- **Current Status**: Active, producing quarterly reports to Congress
- **Public Reports**: 2 historical reviews published (2023-2024)

**Cross-Check**: Pentagon press releases and Congressional testimony confirm AARO's continued operation despite broader disclosure setbacks.

### 3. Whistleblower Protections
**Verified Fact**: UAP Disclosure Act would have provided federal whistleblower protections
- **Status**: NOT IMPLEMENTED - provision removed from final NDAA 2024
- **Impact**: Witnesses like David Grusch remain without specific legal protection

**Cross-Check**: Legal analysis confirms current whistleblower laws insufficient for classified UAP information disclosure.

### 4. Historical Record Review
**Verified Fact**: AARO completed first comprehensive historical review (March 2023)
- **Finding**: No evidence of extraterrestrial activity (contested)
- **Transparency**: Full report publicly released
- **Dispute**: Several witnesses dispute AARO's conclusions

**Cross-Check**: Congressional hearings featured testimony contradicting AARO's findings.

### 5. Classified Information Barriers
**Verified Fact**: Most significant UAP information remains classified
- **Reason**: National security classification systems
- **Access**: Severely restricted even within government
- **Tracking**: No public mechanism to monitor classification status

**Cross-Check**: Multiple intelligence officials confirm "sources and methods" justification for continued secrecy.

### 6. Congressional Oversight
**Verified Fact**: Gang of Eight and Intelligence Committees receive classified briefings
- **Scope**: Limited to oversight members
- **Transparency**: No public disclosure mechanism for findings
- **Accountability**: Limited public verification possible

**Cross-Check**: Congressional record confirms closed-door briefings occur regularly.

### 7. Private Sector Tracking
**Verified Fact**: Non-governmental organizations attempting independent tracking
- **Examples**: UAP research organizations, journalists, academics
- **Limitations**: No access to classified information
- **Filing**: FOIA requests and lawsuits ongoing

**Cross-Check**: Multiple lawsuits confirm government resistance to disclosure beyond minimum legal requirements.

### 8. Timeline Reality Check
**Verified Fact**: No scheduled full disclosure date exists
- **Barrier**: Classification system provides indefinite secrecy
- **Exception**: Congressional action could override classification
- **Prospect**: Remote given political resistance
