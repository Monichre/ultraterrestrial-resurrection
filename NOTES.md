## Note [2025-08-14 15:58:32]

The term that Jacques Vallée coined, which is similar to "invisible college," is indeed "The Invisible College."

In the 1960s, Jacques Vallée, along with astronomer J. Allen Hynek and other academics, formed an informal group of UFO researchers who held regular discussion meetings about UFO phenomena. J. Allen Hynek referred to this group as "The Invisible College." The name is a reference to the original "Invisible College" from the mid-seventeenth century, which was a secretive group of natural philosophers who later formed the Royal Society of London. They operated informally and were united by their shared scientific interests outside of formal institutions.

In Vallée's context, "The Invisible College" described this network of researchers dedicated to studying UFOs, operating somewhat under the radar, much like the historical original who shared knowledge informally among themselves.

#### 11. Rube MCP Server

- **Name:** Rube (by Composio)
- **Source URL:** [Rube MCP Server](https://rube.app/) | [GitHub](https://github.com/ComposioHQ/Rube)
- **Purpose:** Unified MCP server connecting AI agents to 500+ apps (Gmail, Slack, Notion, GitHub, Airtable, etc.) via natural language commands; works across Claude, Cursor, VSCode, and any MCP client.
- **Config Example:**

  ```json
  {
    "mcpServers": {
      "rube": {
        "type": "streamableHttp",
        "url": "https://rube.app/mcp"
      }
    }
  }
  ```

  - *Setup*: Authenticate in-browser, connect apps via OAuth/API keys, supports team/shared access.
  - *Tools*: All Composio connectors (tasks, email, docs, calendar, CRM, code, and more).
