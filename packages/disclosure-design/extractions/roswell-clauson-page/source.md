## Aesthetics analysis (per `prompts/aesthetics.md`)

**Aspects/Subjects/Motifs:** a single faded newspaper page — tall column grid still legible as ruled verticals, but the ink has ghosted almost entirely back into the paper; only scattered word-islands survive ("Phillies", "River", "view", "HALL", "been"), plus a faint halftone photograph dissolving mid-page and a redacted/white-edged hole upper-left where a clipping was lost.

**Adjectives:** spectral, degraded, archival, hushed, palimpsestic, time-ravaged, grey-on-grey.

**Display/Medium/Usage-Context:** scan/render of an aged newsprint page — presented flat as an evidence artifact; the Roswell-adjacent "found document" register the vault's dossier work draws on.

**Genres/Styles:** archival degradation aesthetic; found-document / palimpsest; newsprint noir drained of black; cousin to `case-file-dossier-covers` but where those are manila-and-ink *folders*, this is the *page inside* — the thing time nearly erased.

**Color Palette:** almost achromatic — silvered paper `#c5c5bd` (18.9% share, pixel ✅) against the ghost-ink grey `#3d4143` (21.0%, the dominant share and chroma "peak" at a mere 0.09 sat) with mid haze `#9da19f` (13.3%) carrying the halftone photograph; median `#c3c3bb`. No color accent survives.

**Composition:** portrait broadsheet; rigid multi-column skeleton holding a page whose content has mostly evaporated — structure outliving information. The lost-clipping hole sits upper-left like a wound.

**Emotional Impact:** history failing to be read — the melancholy of evidence degraded past legibility; quiet, haunted, patient.

**Other Details:** distinct within the dossier register as the *most erased* artifact — the design token here is absence: contrast collapsed from newsprint's ~10:1 to a spectral ~6:1, ink islands floating in fog.
