# Design Analysis — Resplendent Mind (Cosmic Apparition Plate)

> Vision-written gold pass. Date: 2026-08-13. Emphasis: scale / the sublime.

## Source

- **Source type**: local image · **Path**: `extractions/resplendent-mind-plate/resplendent-mind-plate.png` (1232×928)
- **Capture method**: direct vision
- **Detected limitations**: nebula filigree and figure detail soft at this resolution; described at the level of mass, light and scale.

## TL;DR

A lone figure stands before a colossal skull-like cosmic apparition built of nebula, lightning and star-field, pale beams raking the rock plain. The vault's most figurative-narrative dark plate — awe with dread baked in.

## 1. Visual identity

**Personality**: sublime, ominous, reverent, vast.
**Mood**: worshipful terror.
**Detectable stylistic references**: Friedrich's *Wanderer above the Sea of Fog*, 70s sci-fi paperback covers, cosmic horror key art, modern film concept.
**Information density**: moderate — one dominant apparition, one figure, one plain; detail lives in the nebula.
**Implicit positioning**: the narrative ceiling of the dark cluster — proof the language can stage a scene, not just a texture.
**Confidence**: ✅ high.

### 1.2 Brand voice

The brand can do reverence. It is willing to make the viewer small — to stage the encounter between a person and something immeasurably larger, and not resolve whether it is god or mirror.

### 1.3 The ONE brand thing

- **The thing**: scale contrast — a tiny human against a frame-filling cosmic head, tied together by the beam of bone-tan light.
- **Why it carries the brand**: it literalizes the vault's core theme (mind confronting the unknown) in a single stageable image.
- **How everything else supports it**: the near-black void and slate plain strip away everything except figure, entity and light.
- **Where it appears**: hero key art, book/report covers, launch moments, "enter the system" splash. ⚠️ medium (usage inferred).

## 3. Components inventory

Signature: **sublime-encounter key art** (tiny figure + towering entity + light beam). Generic: none — illustration, not UI.

## 4. Layout & composition

Near-square. Apparition occupies upper two-thirds as a vertical mass slightly off-center; figure at bottom-center on a diagonal lit path that pulls the eye up; beams radiate downward, inverting the usual "light from above" into "light from the entity". Strong vertical axis, heavy top, deliberate smallness below.

## 5. Reconstruction notes

As UI: asset-class. The transferable mechanic is the *scale axis* — a full-bleed hero with a single small human anchor at bottom-center and a vast textured mass above; the token palette (`void` → `slate-plain` → `bone-light`) is a ready dark-hero scheme. Tricky bits: the light must read warm against cold slate; cooling it to white kills the reverence.

| Layer | Confidence | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Identity | ✅ | figure + apparition unambiguous |
| Colors | ✅ | pixel-grounded four-swatch dark scheme |
| Typography | — | no type present |

## 6. Do's and Don'ts

**Do** — use full-bleed; keep the figure small; let the void occupy real area; pair with a single restrained title.

**Don't** — don't brighten the void; don't enlarge the figure; don't add UI chrome over the apparition's face; don't cool the bone-light toward white.
