# Design Analysis — Daedalus Line Geometry

> Vision-written gold pass. Date: 2026-08-13. Emphasis: mood / art direction.

## Source

- **Source type**: local image · **Path**: `extractions/daedalus-line-geometry/daedalus-line-geometry.png` (928×1232)
- **Capture method**: direct vision
- **Detected limitations**: finest construction lines near-invisible at this scale; the read is system-level (circles, arcs, guides), not every scribe.

## TL;DR

A classical head drawn as a proof: graphite line with all its proportional scaffolding — circles, arcs, center-lines — left visible. The daedalus cluster's analytic plate.

## 1. Visual identity

**Personality**: scholastic, exacting, methodical, luminous-pale.
**Mood**: watching a mind measure beauty.
**Detectable stylistic references**: Dürer/Leonardo proportion studies, treatise illustrations, atelier pedagogical plates.
**Information density**: moderate — one head, one geometry system, generous white.
**Implicit positioning**: the process-revealing twin of daedalus-hatched-bust; theorem vs statue.
**Confidence**: ✅ high.

### 1.2 Brand voice

Mastery shown, not claimed. The design believes the audience trusts craft it can *see working* — that leaving the construction lines in is a kind of honesty, and that beauty demonstrated as geometry is more persuasive than beauty asserted.

### 1.3 The ONE brand thing

- **The thing**: visible scaffolding — the construction geometry is not erased but elevated to co-subject.
- **Why it carries the brand**: it converts a portrait into a treatise; remove the arcs and it's a competent drawing, keep them and it's a worldview.
- **How everything else supports it**: palette stays at paper-and-graphite whisper; no hatch, no fill, no ornament competes with the line system.
- **Where it appears**: methodology plates, about/process pages, treatise covers. ⚠️ medium (usage inferred).

## 4. Layout & composition

Portrait 3:4; head centered slightly low; construction arcs radiating into margins; uniform fine line weight; hierarchy by density.

## 5. Reconstruction notes

As UI: asset-class plate (hybrid potential: SVG geometry over raster figure). Quick wins: whisper palette tolerates any light page. Tricky bits: the guides must *fit* the head's proportions — decorative circles that don't construct anything read as fraud to this audience.

## 6. Do's and Don'ts

**Do** — keep the palette at paper/graphite whisper; let arcs bleed into margins; pair with serif treatise type if typeset.

**Don't** — don't ink the construction lines heavier than the figure; don't add hatch or shading; don't center-crop the arcs away.
