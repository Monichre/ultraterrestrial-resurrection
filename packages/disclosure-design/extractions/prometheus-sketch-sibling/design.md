> Vision-written gold pass. Date: 2026-08-13. Emphasis: mood / art direction.

## TL;DR

The mid-grey, human register of the Prometheus set: a gestural figure study with smudged shading instead of hatching or geometry. Where the siblings measure the Titan, this one reaches for him.

## 1. Visual identity

**Personality**: gestural, intimate, searching, unresolved.
**Mood**: the working page; thought in motion.
**Detectable stylistic references**: life-drawing atelier, sketchbook studies, academic figure drawing.
**Information density**: low-moderate — one figure, tonal atmosphere.
**Implicit positioning**: the process-art counterpoint to the emblem plates.
**Confidence**: ✅ high.

### 1.2 Brand voice

Permission to be unfinished. The smudge is honest labor; the brand trusts the viewer with work-in-progress rather than only polished outcomes.

### 1.3 The ONE brand thing

- **The thing**: smudge-shaded gestural figure on mid-grey paper.
- **Why it carries the brand**: it is the human hand inside the otherwise diagrammatic system.
- **How everything else supports it**: achromatic palette keeps focus on pressure and gesture.
- **Where it appears**: process sections, about/craft pages, interstitials. ⚠️ medium (usage inferred).

## 4. Layout & composition

## 5. Reconstruction notes

As UI: asset-class. Pairs with `--color-bg-secondary` grey shells; do not pair with the near-black register without a tonal bridge. Quick wins: three-value achromatic palette. Tricky bits: smudge texture is raster-only.
