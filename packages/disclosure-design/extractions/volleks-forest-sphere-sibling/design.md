# Design Analysis — Volleks Forest Sphere (Sibling)

> Vision-written gold pass. Date: 2026-08-13. Emphasis: mood / art direction.

## Source

- **Source type**: local image · **Path**: `extractions/volleks-forest-sphere-sibling/volleks-forest-sphere-sibling.png` (2912×1632)
- **Capture method**: direct vision
- **Detected limitations**: the sphere's inner swirl is denser than words — nebula detail summarized, not mapped.

## TL;DR

A luminous translucent sphere held in a night valley — waterfalls, mirror-stream, aurora. The sphere cluster's sanctuary register: cosmic object as hidden holy thing, lit from within.

## 1. Visual identity

**Personality**: numinous, lush, enchanted, pristine.
**Mood**: discovery without conquest — a held breath.
**Detectable stylistic references**: astral-projection poster art, fantasy matte painting, Unreal-Engine cinematic stills.
**Information density**: moderate — many natural elements organized into one devotional axis.
**Implicit positioning**: the emotional peak of the sphere cluster; wonder at full saturation.
**Confidence**: ✅ high.

### 1.2 Brand voice

Reverence for the hidden. The design believes the audience wants magic that feels *undisturbed* — the sphere isn't landed or attacking, it's sleeping; every element (water, trees, aurora) genuflects toward it.

### 1.3 The ONE brand thing

- **The thing**: the sphere as internal light source — translucent shell, nebula within, the valley lit by what it holds.
- **Why it carries the brand**: it's the cluster's thesis in one object — the same sphere that's matte evidence in the Roosevelt frame is here a lantern; the object is constant, the worldview changes.
- **How everything else supports it**: waterfalls, stream, and treeline all mirror or funnel toward the glow; the sky stays subordinate.
- **Where it appears**: key art, covers, ambient hero loops. ⚠️ medium (usage inferred).

## 3. Components inventory

Signature: **cradled-sphere valley** (V-funnel slopes + central glow + waterfall axis + mirror stream). Generic: none — art, not UI.

## 4. Layout & composition

Ultrawide landscape, perfect central symmetry soft-broken by the stream's meander; sphere centered; vertical waterfall accent directly beneath; trees as repoussoir.

## 5. Reconstruction notes

As UI: asset-class immersive hero. Quick wins: dark navy page blend (`#131a27`) lets edges dissolve. Tricky bits: inner-sphere nebula + translucency need layered radial gradients and glow; reflection in the stream must be *dimmer* than the source or the illusion dies.

## 6. Do's and Don'ts

**Do** — preserve central symmetry; keep the glow cyan-aqua against navy; let edges fall to `#131a27` for seamless page embedding.

**Don't** — don't warm the palette; don't add figures or structures; don't over-brighten the sky past the sphere.
