## Texture Generation Prompts

### Aged Paper

```text
seamless aged archival paper texture with yellowed fibers, subtle stains, dust specks and worn corners, suitable for UI backgrounds
```

### Fold Overlay

```text
transparent vertical paper fold seam with subtle crease shadows and document wear, no text or objects
```

### Photocopy Noise

```text
high-contrast black toner noise texture with dust and scratches, transparent overlay for photocopy decay
```

### Halftone Field

```text
coarse black halftone dot texture on transparent background to simulate analog print degradation
```

### Burn Edge

```text
singed archival paper edge with soot, smoke stains and heat discoloration, isolated edge texture
```

### Redaction Strips

```text
black redaction bars with irregular photocopied edges and varying lengths
```
