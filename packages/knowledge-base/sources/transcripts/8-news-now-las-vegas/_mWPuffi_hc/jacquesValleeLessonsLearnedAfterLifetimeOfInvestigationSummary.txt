### Topic: The Control System Hypothesis
- **Unique Title:** Control System Hypothesis (Vallée)
- **Summary:** A theoretical framework proposing that the UFO/UAP phenomenon functions as an environmental "control system" that modulates human belief, behavior, and cultural development over long timescales. Vallée distinguishes this from a conventional physical control system (e.g., a thermostat); it is an *open* system that permits exit and adaptation, analogous to a university (structured but escapable) versus a concentration camp (closed). The hypothesis is attributed by Vallée to **Jacques Bergier** and others, not originated by Vallée himself.
- **Visual Evidence:** No
- **Linked SMEs:** Jacques Vallée, Jacques Bergier (historical attribution)

- **Confidence:** High (directly stated by Vallée)
