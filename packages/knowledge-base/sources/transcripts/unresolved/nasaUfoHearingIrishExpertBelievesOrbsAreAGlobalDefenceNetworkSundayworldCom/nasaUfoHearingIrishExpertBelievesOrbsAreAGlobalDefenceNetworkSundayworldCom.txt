An Irishman who has been studying UFOs for more than 20 years says he believes the phenomenon are a “global defence network.”

NASA held their first ever public hearing in Washington this week to discuss sightings of UFOs, or ‘unidentified aerial phenomena (UAPs)’.

The four-hour meeting, which made headlines across the globe, heard details of how metallic orb UFOs have been “spotted all over the world”.

Pentagon chief Sean Kirkpatrick admitted during the NASA hearing that the orbs have been seen “making very interesting manoeuvres.”

Now, one Irishman Patrick Jackson (45) says he believes he potentially has the answer to the mystery and says he has the backing of retired US Air Force pilots and IT specialists.

Patrick, who has previously written a book on the topic called ‘Quantum Paranormal’, told the [Sunday World](https://www.sundayworld.com/) he believes the UFOs have a defensive purpose.

![NASA hearing](https://focus.sundayworld.com/thumbor/VlWFqNbGoDHR8BZrzMhRe3On6Do=/0x0:761x375/fit-in/960x720/prod-mh-ireland/420b34f7-1b80-40b3-b171-ecd266df4dd3/3d4dfbce-3211-4437-9699-c388e7ec2bbe/420b34f7-1b80-40b3-b171-ecd266df4dd3.jpg)NASA hearing

“The spheres behave like a global defence network that intercept space based threats ranging from meteors to other space based threats.

"Once I figured this out – I got the shakes, it was so overwhelming,” Patrick says from his home in Cambridge.

This week, the NASA hearing was told how around 800 reports of UFOs collected over decades were examined.

And while the majority could be explained, a fraction – between two to five percent – are truly unexplained.

At the meeting, officials did not rule out the possibility that the objects are extra-terrestrial in origin.

![Footage of an orb UFO](https://focus.sundayworld.com/thumbor/NJIkrlcO-I-6oiKybNCNXDjuJJU=/225x0:815x443/960x720/prod-mh-ireland/ff4fa5c8-78ed-4d9d-ba7f-be69029d90e5/2acec2c5-e1ad-431c-b0d6-c7df4b35bfcf/ff4fa5c8-78ed-4d9d-ba7f-be69029d90e5.jpg)Footage of an orb UFO

NASA UAP chief David Spergel also called for the “stigma” to be removed from experts investigating UFOS, claiming it was hindering scientific progress.

Speaking to the [Sunday World](https://www.sundayworld.com/service/app-download), Mr Jackson – an IT database specialist – says he honed his skills on trying to understand poltergeist activity in a famous location – 30 East Drive, home to the most ‘aggressive poltergeist’ in the UK.

“I observed silver spheres flying around the house, tennis ball sized drones, intelligently controlled.

”Like marbles bouncing off the walls, hitting my face.

![Patrick Jackson](https://focus.sundayworld.com/thumbor/9A0YizYthp8n404k-4o0yfj8q6o=/0x0:302x367/fit-in/960x720/prod-mh-ireland/1917272f-5e43-4c02-92ba-e4cac6fad227/8eb80630-2814-445a-b11f-28a8f6bdb2f7/1917272f-5e43-4c02-92ba-e4cac6fad227.jpg)Patrick Jackson

“The behaviour patterns observed in the building were not human like - but machine like.

"I saw these orbs everywhere - the flight behaviour was unusual, they would repeat the same patterns and processes.

“It’s still amazing to watch. I have all these videos - nowadays I have figured out how to trigger it and why the system operates the way it does,” Patrick says.

The Irishman’s theory includes the idea that some element of poltergeist activity is, in fact, radiation emitted from these spheres.

Patrick – who has been studying these orbs for two decades- states there are three different types of spheres.

![Patrick's theory](https://focus.sundayworld.com/thumbor/3joPk9-LmT2s9BDnHb76GnneVYc=/0x0:1310x781/fit-in/960x720/prod-mh-ireland/702b238c-1f3a-4b45-87a8-580a5d8ce7fa/17416bce-48da-44fb-a61b-1132cb4575a6/702b238c-1f3a-4b45-87a8-580a5d8ce7fa.jpg)Patrick's theory

“Type three operate in buildings and are the underlying communication network. They relay signals to each other”, he claims.

“It is a scarecrow-like action designed to move people away from emissions.

"You may hear a door banging in another part of the house. This action is designed to move people away, the further away a person is, the safer it is.

“People feel sick or faint when investigating this, it is not the result of ‘dead people’ but radiation bursts,” says Patrick.

While this might sound crazy to some, Patrick’s work has attracted the attention of top scientists worldwide. 

US Air Force pilot, Joe Albegg, says Patrick’s research is “on a level never seen before.” Patrick further details how these UFO spheres operate.

![Retired US Air Fore Pilot Joe Albregg](https://focus.sundayworld.com/thumbor/IpUvDTXDI7VkqmJiMdutV0f0yU0=/0x0:400x413/fit-in/960x720/prod-mh-ireland/6156d7f5-6f4e-4d09-8143-d8235be6739f/1d90cc0d-1852-479a-9250-69eb1154f228/6156d7f5-6f4e-4d09-8143-d8235be6739f.jpg)Retired US Air Fore Pilot Joe Albregg

“The type three spheres are in buildings and operate 5-10 miles apart, in lines and clusters around environmental topology.

“It acts like a microwave based relay system. They send a signal to the type two which the US Navy are seeing. Those operate 300 feet off the ground, where needed.

“There is the type one in the sky which triangulate on other incoming UFOs.

"They seem to make a V formation and yes, I suspect that, they are protecting humans and I suspect, have been doing so for thousands of years.”

![Spheres in history](https://focus.sundayworld.com/thumbor/37-BeoWRqXGb1ehCy7H0L5TwZq0=/0x0:898x674/fit-in/960x720/prod-mh-ireland/30408d57-9525-464c-a5fb-0259ed3b21fd/44566094-bc35-44bc-95cf-4359bfe24e36/30408d57-9525-464c-a5fb-0259ed3b21fd.jpg)Spheres in history

Last month, the Pentagon held a public hearing on the topic. One slide released on UAP reporting trends from 1996-2023 provided specific detail about these objects.

Their appearance was described as being round with a size of one to four meters. Usual colour: white, silver or translucent.

The performance of these UAP was at an altitude between 10,000 – 30,000 feet. Velocity from ‘stationary to Mach 2’.

No thermal exhaust has ever been detected. The orbs have radio signatures, radar signatures and are visible through infrared. Patrick has specific details and evidence on these orbs.

![Pentagon slide](https://focus.sundayworld.com/thumbor/WidGHVtZwFVj9A_tyFWoM2TRG7Y=/0x0:291x443/fit-in/960x720/prod-mh-ireland/53c4a926-5690-4c37-8209-eeb712d60e0a/3112f845-7401-49a6-b2be-f039c60cd6f6/53c4a926-5690-4c37-8209-eeb712d60e0a.jpg)Pentagon slide

“When the target is above, the orbs form a V. When in a flat triangle, the target is below.

“You see it again and again. Type two were seen over Mosul by the US military. They flew over the Queen’s jubilee, there’s video.

“They appear to all have the same flight behaviour- hypersonic speeds, they stop, sit in the same spot for a time - operating like satellites in reverse.

“People may feel or faint in ‘haunted houses’ because of the radiation these objects may things give out. My working model is that it is actually a global defence network that intercepts space-based threats ranging from meteors to other threats.

![Theory](https://focus.sundayworld.com/thumbor/T5ZHvJEMJ60-gV3h9MG8Mp6SC3I=/0x0:1012x858/fit-in/960x720/prod-mh-ireland/1521c57e-3b56-4278-8f13-4a1b300a6914/8df3fc32-e63e-4bda-bbcb-f16cba9569e1/1521c57e-3b56-4278-8f13-4a1b300a6914.jpg)Theory

“You see them as triangles in the sky, in the same position, until they need to change configuration.

"They will intercept all threats,” Patrick says.

The obvious question is who exactly is operating these spheres, which Patrick believes have been protecting humans for a long time.

"It can only come through civilian sources - it can’t come from the government. When it comes from a civilian who has never been in a programme, nobody cares.

“Government are very sticky about this. I doubt the Irish government are aware, it is US based at the moment.

"The smart people likely know this is right. The type three are a communication network. UFOs leave or risk being taken out.

![Theory](https://focus.sundayworld.com/thumbor/QbTEQp2c_rwir2AMgoCSqd1CkQY=/0x0:1446x654/fit-in/960x720/prod-mh-ireland/ed96b0a2-c4e9-4825-8bf9-1b212111c911/2b51c8fd-17bc-44f0-aafe-df064599b915/ed96b0a2-c4e9-4825-8bf9-1b212111c911.jpg)Theory

“I’m very confident about this, albeit we need more data to study. The type one-V formation is visible throughout history. These are the foo fighters seen during WW2. Air shows trigger them.

“It seems to be thousands of years old. This one thing that may be trying to keep external threats out. The system is working at three levels and may be responsible for poltergeist activity and so-called ball lightning.”

If Patrick’s theory is correct, it would be similar to Christopher Columbus discovering America.

“Yes, a slight shock in the beginning, it will settle and that will be it,” he revealed.
