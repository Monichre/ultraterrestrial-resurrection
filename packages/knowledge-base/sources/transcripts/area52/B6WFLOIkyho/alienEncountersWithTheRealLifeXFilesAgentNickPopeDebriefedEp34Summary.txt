### Topic: The Calvine UFO Case (1990)
- **Summary:** A 1990 incident in Scotland where two witnesses photographed a large, diamond-shaped craft hovering in the sky, allegedly being observed by a Royal Air Force jet. The Ministry of Defence (MOD) investigated the case. The photographs were analyzed by the Joint Air Reconnaissance Intelligence Centre (JARIC), which concluded the object was a solid, structured craft approximately 75 feet across. The case gained renewed public attention when a former RAF press officer, Craig Lindsay, released a copy of one of the original photographs.
- **Visual Evidence:** Yes (six photographs, one of which was released by Craig Lindsay).
- **Related Entities:** Personnel (Nick Pope, Craig Lindsay), Events (Calvine UFO Case), Documents (Photographs, MOD files).
