# US Astronauts Speak on UFOs and Extraterrestrial Life

**From Mercury to the ISS, American space explorers have reported unexplained phenomena and shared beliefs about alien visitation - here's the complete documented record of their testimonies.**

The UFO phenomenon has captivated public imagination for decades, but when trained astronauts - individuals selected for their exceptional observational skills and scientific rigor - report unexplained sightings or express beliefs about extraterrestrial visitation, their testimonies carry unique weight. This comprehensive investigation documents every verified statement made by US astronauts about UFOs, UAPs, and extraterrestrial life, from the earliest days of the Mercury program through today's International Space Station missions.

Our research reveals a spectrum of perspectives: from **Gordon Cooper's multiple UFO encounters** and **Edgar Mitchell's conviction about alien visitation** to **Scott Kelly's scientific skepticism** as a member of NASA's UAP study team. While some astronauts describe personal experiences that remain unexplained, others advocate for rigorous investigation while maintaining that extraordinary claims require extraordinary evidence. The following profiles present each astronaut's statements in full context, with verified sources and official NASA portraits where available.

## The believers: astronauts convinced of alien presence

### Edgar Mitchell (Apollo 14) - The Moon Walker Who Believed

**Mission Details:** Lunar Module Pilot, Apollo 14 (February 1971), 6th person to walk on the Moon
**Military Rank:** Captain, U.S. Navy (retired)
**Academic Credentials:** BS Industrial Management (Carnegie Tech), BS Aeronautical Engineering (Naval Postgraduate School), ScD Aeronautics/Astronautics (MIT)
**Post-NASA:** Founded Institute of Noetic Sciences (1973)

Edgar Mitchell became the most prominent astronaut advocate for UFO disclosure, though he never claimed a personal sighting. His most famous statement came in a **2008 interview on Kerrang Radio**: "I happen to have been privileged enough to be in on the fact that we've been visited on this planet, and the UFO phenomenon is real." He estimated with **"90 percent" certainty** that many UFOs "belong to visitors from other planets."

In a **2015 interview with the UK Mirror**, Mitchell elaborated on extraterrestrial motivations: "White Sands was a testing ground for atomic weapons—and that's what the extraterrestrials were interested in... The ETs had been attempting to keep us from going to war and help create peace on Earth." He claimed unnamed Pentagon sources and Roswell witnesses had privately confirmed alien visitation to him.

However, Mitchell maintained intellectual honesty about his lack of direct experience. In a **2014 AskMen interview**, he clarified that claims about a worldwide UFO coverup were "just speculation on my part" and acknowledged he had never personally seen a UFO. His wife Anita (married 1973-1984) later revealed that "many of the astronauts" shared similar beliefs based on unexplained phenomena they'd witnessed.

**Primary Sources:**
- CNN Larry King Live (2008)
- Kerrang Radio interview (July 23, 2008)
- Daily Mirror interview (2015)
- Disclosure Project testimony (1998)

### Gordon Cooper (Mercury 9, Gemini 5) - The Fighter Pilot's Multiple Encounters

**Mission Details:** Mercury-Atlas 9 "Faith 7" (May 1963), Gemini 5 (August 1965)
**Military Rank:** Colonel, U.S. Air Force
**Academic Background:** BS Aeronautical Engineering, distinguished test pilot

Gordon Cooper reported more UFO encounters than any other astronaut, spanning his military and NASA careers. His most dramatic account involved a **1957 incident at Edwards Air Force Base** where his film crew allegedly captured footage of a metallic saucer landing: "It was a classic saucer, shiny silver and smooth, about 30 feet across. It was pretty clear it was an alien craft." The film was reportedly sent to Washington and never seen again.

Cooper's first encounter occurred in **1951 over Germany** while flying F-86 fighters. He and fellow pilots observed "metallic, saucer-shaped vehicles at great altitudes" that could outmaneuver their jets. During his **Mercury 9 mission in 1963**, Cooper reported seeing a glowing green object trailing his capsule, which he claimed was captured on film but subsequently classified.

In **1978 testimony before a UN panel** chaired by Kurt Waldheim, Cooper stated unequivocally: "I believe that these extraterrestrial vehicles and their crews are visiting this planet from other planets which obviously are a little more technically advanced than we are here on Earth." He maintained these beliefs throughout his life, telling the **National Enquirer in 1997**: "I know other astronauts share my feelings... And we know the government is sitting on hard evidence of UFOs."

**Primary Sources:**
- UN testimony (1978)
- Autobiography "Leap of Faith" (2000)
- "Out of the Blue" documentary (2003)
- Merv Griffin Show appearances

### Brian O'Leary (NASA Astronaut Group 6) - The Scientist Who Went Rogue

**Mission Details:** Selected 1967 for cancelled Mars mission, resigned 1968
**Academic Background:** BA Physics (Williams), PhD Astronomy (UC Berkeley)
**Post-NASA:** Cornell and Princeton professor, consciousness researcher

Though O'Leary never flew in space, his transformation from mainstream scientist to UFO disclosure advocate made him controversial. At the **1994 International Forum on New Science**, he declared: "For nearly 50 years, the secrecy apparatus within the United States Government has kept from the public UFO and alien contact information." He called it a "cosmic Watergate" that made conventional scandals "look like child's play."

O'Leary went further than most, claiming knowledge of specific alien technologies: "These visitors use consciousness-influencing technologies – the toroid and use co-rotating magnetic disks for their propulsion systems." His credibility suffered when he appeared in the **2001 Fox special "Conspiracy Theory: Did We Land on the Moon?"** suggesting the lunar landings might have been faked.

**Primary Sources:**
- International Forum on New Science testimony (1994)
- Project Camelot interviews
- Thrive Movement statements

## The witnesses: unexplained phenomena in space

### Story Musgrave (Six Space Shuttle Missions) - The Snake in Space

**Mission Details:** STS-6, STS-51-F, STS-33, STS-44, STS-61, STS-80 (1983-1996)
**Unique Achievement:** Only astronaut to fly on all 5 Space Shuttles
**Academic Background:** Medical doctor with 6 academic degrees

Story Musgrave, one of NASA's most experienced astronauts, reported seeing inexplicable objects on multiple missions. In a **1995 interview for the TV series "Sightings"**, he described: "On two of my missions, and I still don't have an answer, I have seen a snake out there, six, seven, eight feet long. It must have been elastic, because it seemed to have internal waves in it, and it followed us for quite a long time."

At the **2011 MUFON Symposium**, Musgrave clarified his position: while he believes extraterrestrial life exists throughout the universe, he doubts they've visited Earth. When showing slides in astronomy presentations featuring an alien "Grey," he would tell audiences: "These guys are real, I assure you!" - though the context suggested he meant aliens exist somewhere, not necessarily here.

### Leland Melvin (Space Shuttle Atlantis) - From Ice to Orbs

**Mission Details:** STS-122 (2008), STS-129 (2009)
**Unique Background:** Former NFL player (Detroit Lions draft pick)
**Post-NASA:** NASA Associate Administrator for Education (2010-2014)

Melvin's UFO-related statements span from space to Earth. During a shuttle mission, he spotted something unusual, which he described in an **August 2018 Twitter exchange**: "I thought I saw something organic/alien like floating out of the payload bay." He and crewmate Randy Bresnik called ground control, who identified it as ice from Freon hoses. When asked if NASA might have lied, Melvin responded cryptically: "Hmmm. Don't think so, but you never know."

More recently in **2024**, Melvin reported a terrestrial encounter: two metallic spherical orbs, approximately 3 feet in diameter, flew within 20 feet of his private aircraft over Texas. The objects weren't detected on radar, adding to a growing list of similar pilot reports.

### James McDivitt (Gemini 4, Apollo 9) - The Cylindrical Mystery

**Mission Details:** Commander Gemini 4 (June 1965), Commander Apollo 9 (March 1969)
**Military Rank:** Brigadier General, U.S. Air Force

During Gemini 4, McDivitt observed and photographed an unidentified object: "It had a very definite shape - a cylindrical object - it was white - it had a long arm that stuck out on the side... like a beer can or a pop can, and with a little thing like maybe like a pencil or something sticking out of it."

The **University of Colorado's Condon Report** cited McDivitt's sighting as one of three astronaut observations that "have not been adequately explained." While McDivitt himself suggested it might have been space debris, he firmly rejected proposed explanations when shown photos of the Titan II booster by skeptic Philip Klass: "I am sure that this is not a photograph of the object which I described many times."

## The skeptics: scientific explanations prevail

### Scott Kelly (ISS Year-Long Mission) - The Evidence Seeker

**Mission Details:** Four space missions including ISS Expedition 45/46 (year in space)
**Current Role:** NASA UAP Independent Study Team member (2022-2023)

Scott Kelly represents the scientific skeptic position among astronauts. As a member of NASA's UAP study team, he explained at the **May 31, 2023 meeting** how atmospheric optical illusions can create UFO sightings in space. He emphasized: "What was it Carl Sagan said, 'extraordinary claims require extraordinary evidence'? They have every right to make extraordinary claims, but without the extraordinary evidence, they're just claims."

Kelly shared a revealing anecdote about a military flight where radar operators thought they'd encountered a UFO - it turned out to be a "Bart Simpson balloon." While maintaining healthy skepticism, he stated: "I would like to believe they're true. But I can't until someone shows me the real evidence."

### Chris Hadfield (ISS Commander) - The Rational Observer

**Mission Details:** Three space flights, ISS Expedition 35 Commander
**Nationality:** Canadian (worked extensively with NASA)

Hadfield takes perhaps the strongest skeptical stance. In a **May 2021 CBC Radio interview** responding to Pentagon UFO reports, he stated: "Obviously, I've seen countless things in the sky that I don't understand. But to see something in the sky that you don't understand and then to immediately conclude that it's intelligent life from another solar system is the height of foolishness and lack of logic."

While believing extraterrestrial life likely exists elsewhere, Hadfield maintains: "Definitively up to this point, we have found no evidence of life anywhere except Earth, and we're looking."

### Tom Jones (Four Space Shuttle Missions) - The Debunker

**Mission Details:** STS-59, STS-68, STS-80, STS-98 (1994-2001)
**Background:** Planetary scientist, Air Force Academy graduate

Jones actively debunks UFO claims involving astronauts. Regarding famous STS-80 footage showing mysterious objects, he explained: "A few ice crystals or flakes of thruster residue in the near field are floating by, get hit by a thruster exhaust plume and zip out of the scene."

He stated definitively: "I don't think I have heard of anybody, in the astronaut corps, who has ever seen anything that they thought was unexplained." Jones emphasizes that astronauts face no classification restrictions preventing UFO discussions, countering conspiracy theories about enforced silence.

## The clarifiers: misunderstood encounters explained

### Buzz Aldrin (Apollo 11) - Setting the Record Straight

**Mission Details:** Lunar Module Pilot, Apollo 11 (July 1969), 2nd person on Moon
**Military Rank:** Colonel, U.S. Air Force
**Academic Background:** BS West Point, ScD Astronautics (MIT)

Aldrin's Apollo 11 "UFO" sighting became one of the most misrepresented astronaut stories. In his **2014 Reddit AMA**, he explained: "On Apollo 11 in route to the Moon, I observed a light out the window that appeared to be moving alongside us." However, he clarified: "It was either the rocket we had separated from, or the 4 panels that moved away when we extracted the lander... I feel absolutely convinced that we were looking at the sun reflected off of one of these panels."

A **2018 tabloid story** claiming Aldrin "passed a lie detector test" about seeing UFOs was thoroughly debunked by his spokesperson. Aldrin maintains a scientific position: "There may be aliens in our Milky Way galaxy, and there are billions of other galaxies. The probability is almost CERTAIN that there is life somewhere in space" - but not necessarily visiting Earth.

### Donald "Deke" Slayton (Original Mercury 7) - The Test Pilot's Report

**Mission Details:** Apollo-Soyuz Test Project (1975)
**Military Rank:** Major, U.S. Air Force
**Incident:** 1951 Minnesota UFO sighting

While testing a P-51 Mustang in 1951, Slayton encountered an unidentified object: "It looked like a saucer, a disk... I tracked it for a little way, and then all of a sudden the damn thing just took off. It pulled about a 45 degree climbing turn and accelerated and just flat disappeared." Ground crews tracked the object with theodolite, computing its speed at over 4,000 MPH. The incident appears in **Project Blue Book files**, lending it official documentation.

### Eugene Cernan (Apollo 17) - Brief But Clear

**Mission Details:** Commander Apollo 17 (December 1972), last person on Moon
**Military Rank:** Captain, U.S. Navy

Cernan made limited UFO statements, but in a **1973 Los Angeles Times interview**, he said clearly: "I've been asked [about UFOs] and I've said publicly I thought they [UFOs] were somebody else, some other civilization."

## Recent developments: The UAP era

### Mark Kelly (Senator, Former Astronaut) - The Policy Maker

**Mission Details:** Four Space Shuttle flights
**Current Role:** U.S. Senator (Arizona), Armed Services Committee

As a senator, Mark Kelly bridges the astronaut and policy worlds. In a **2023 interview**, he stated: "I've seen some compelling testimony from navy fighter pilots who, in one case, in a position of leadership in a squadron have seen something very compelling. It's our obligation on the Armed Services Committee, to investigate it and put more resources behind studying this kind of phenomena."

Despite his space experience, Kelly clarifies: "While I've spent time orbiting the Earth, I haven't [seen anything unusual]." He co-leads bipartisan efforts to fund the Pentagon's All-domain Anomaly Resolution Office (AARO).

### NASA's Official UAP Study

In **2022-2023**, NASA convened an independent UAP study team including astronaut Scott Kelly. The **September 14, 2023 report** found no evidence of extraterrestrial origins for UAPs but recommended better data collection and reduced stigma around reporting. NASA appointed **Mark McInerney** as its first Director of UAP Research, initially keeping his identity secret due to harassment of panel members.

## Patterns and perspectives across the astronaut corps

The astronaut community reveals **three distinct positions** on UFOs and extraterrestrial life:

**Direct Experiencers:** Gordon Cooper and James McDivitt reported personal sightings they couldn't explain, though neither definitively claimed extraterrestrial origin. Their military backgrounds and observational training lend credibility to their accounts.

**Believers Without Sightings:** Edgar Mitchell and Brian O'Leary became prominent UFO disclosure advocates despite lacking personal encounters. They relied on secondhand sources and philosophical beliefs about extraterrestrial visitation.

**Scientific Skeptics:** Scott Kelly, Chris Hadfield, and Tom Jones represent the majority position - maintaining that unusual sightings have conventional explanations while supporting rigorous scientific investigation.

What emerges is not a conspiracy of silence but a **spectrum of individual perspectives** shaped by personal experiences, scientific training, and philosophical beliefs. While some astronauts report genuinely puzzling observations, none provide the "extraordinary evidence" that Carl Sagan famously required for extraordinary claims. The recent establishment of NASA's UAP research office and congressional investigations reflect a growing institutional willingness to study these phenomena seriously - even as the scientific community maintains healthy skepticism about extraterrestrial explanations.
