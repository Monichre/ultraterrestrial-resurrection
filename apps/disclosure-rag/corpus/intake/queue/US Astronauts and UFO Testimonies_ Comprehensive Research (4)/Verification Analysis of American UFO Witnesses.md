## Credibility Assessment Framework

- Tim Gallaudet: Navy Rear Admiral, recent testimony, senior leadership
- David Fravor: Navy Commander, multiple witnesses, radar confirmation
- Karl Nell: Army Colonel, academic-level analysis, UAP Task Force

### High Credibility (2 figures):

- David Grusch: Intelligence background verified, but claims based on secondhand information

## Common Verification Patterns:

### Post-Service Disclosure Pattern:
- All figures made UFO claims after leaving government service
- Similar to international pattern (Paul Hellyer, Haim Eshed)
- Suggests institutional pressure against disclosure while serving

### Multiple Witness Corroboration:
- Fravor/Graves incidents had multiple military witnesses
- Elizondo/Mellon worked together on disclosure efforts
- Grusch claims supported by Karl Nell and "Jonathan Grey"

### Official Documentation:
- Congressional testimony under oath (legal consequences for perjury)
- Pentagon video releases authenticate some claims
- AARO reports acknowledge UAP reality while disputing alien origin

### Institutional Resistance:
- Pentagon disputed Elizondo's role after initial confirmation
- Classification issues with video releases
- Ongoing secrecy despite congressional pressure

## Key Verification Challenges:

### Classification Barriers:
- Many details cannot be publicly verified due to classification
- Witnesses limited in what they can discuss in open hearings
- SCIF (Secure Compartmented Information Facility) sessions for sensitive details

### Secondhand Claims:
- Grusch's most dramatic claims based on documents/conversations
- No direct physical evidence presented publicly
- Reliance on witness credibility rather than material proof

### Government Denials:
- NASA and DoD officially deny extraterrestrial explanations
- AARO reports find no evidence of alien technology
- Tension between witness claims and official positions
