# charlie-kirk-assassination-research — Working Notes

> Compiled: 2026-05-08

## Synthesis

The central tension in the Kirk assassination research is the exploding microphone theory versus the official lone gunman narrative. Jon Bray's "Four Pillars" analysis is technically detailed but comes from an unverified independent analyst with no confirmed forensic credentials. The FBI's tight control of evidence (no autopsy, limited video/audio) feeds the alternative theory ecosystem but isn't itself evidence of a cover-up.

The Joe Kent, AES factory, and Israel angles are the most speculative threads — they form a coherent narrative (foreign intelligence operation, staged weapon, cover-up) but lack any mainstream corroboration.

## Key Distinction

The exploding mic theory and the foreign involvement theory are **logically separate**:
- You can accept the exploding mic theory without accepting Israeli involvement
- You can believe in a cover-up without believing in foreign involvement
- Evidence that proves/disproves one doesn't necessarily affect the other
