## Detailed Findings from Gravity Warp Drive Site

### Additional Astronauts with UFO/UAP Testimonies:

8. **Victor Afanasyev (Soviet Cosmonaut)** - Soyuz 6 mission to Solyut 6 space station, April 1979
   - Quote: "It followed us during half of our orbit. We observed it on the light side, and when we entered the shadow side, it disappeared completely. It was an engineered structure, made from some type of metal, approximately 40 meters long with inner hulls. The object was narrow here and wider here, and inside there were openings. Some places had projections like small wings. The object stayed very close to us. We photographed it, and our photos showed it to be 23 to 28 meters away."

9. **Neil Armstrong (Apollo 11)** - October 1999 statement
   - Quote: "We have no proof. But if we extrapolate, based on the best information we have available to us, we have to come to the conclusion that ... other life probably exists out there and perhaps in many places..."

10. **Scott Carpenter (Mercury-Atlas 7)** - May 24, 1962
    - Quote: "At no time, when the astronauts were in space were they alone: there was a constant surveillance by UFOs."
    - Note: Carpenter photographed a UFO while in orbit. NASA still has not released the photograph.

11. **Eugene Cernan (Apollo 17)** - 1973 Los Angeles Times article
    - Quote: "...I've been asked about UFOs and I've said publicly I thought they were somebody else, some other civilization."

12. **Gordon Cooper (Mercury/Gemini)** - Multiple testimonies
    - UN Panel 1985: "I believe that these extraterrestrial vehicles and their crews are visiting this planet from other planets, which obviously are a little more technically advanced than we are here on Earth."
    - UN Committee testimony: "For many years I have lived with a secret, in a secrecy imposed on all specialists and astronauts. I can now reveal that every day, in the U.S.A., our radar instruments capture objects of form and composition unknown to us."
    - Multiple UFO encounters including:
      - Germany 1950s as military pilot - filmed UFO landing
      - Edwards Air Force Base 1957 - witnessed UFO landing with camera crew
      - Roswell connection - friend revealed alien craft crash and crew recovery

13. **James McDivitt (Gemini 4)** - Orbital encounter
    - Quote: "At one stage we even thought it might be necessary to take evasive action to avoid a collision."
    - Note: Commenting on orbital encounter with "weird object" with arm-like extensions that approached their capsule. Later saw two similar objects over the Caribbean.

14. **John Glenn (Mercury)** 
    - Quote: "I believe certain reports of flying saucers to be legitimate."

15. **James Lovell (Apollo 8/Apollo 13)** - Christmas 1968
    - Quote: "Mission Control, please be informed, there is a Santa Claus."
    - Note: Made this transmission after coming around the far side of the moon on Apollo-8 mission with Frank Borman and William Anders. "Santa Claus" was apparently a codeword for UFO sighting.

16. **Edgar Mitchell (Apollo 14)** - Multiple statements from 1971 onwards
    - 1971: "We all know that UFOs are real. All we need to ask is where do they come from?"
    - Extensive testimony about government secrecy, Roswell incident, and alien visitation
    - Quote: "I've talked with people of stature --- of military and government credentials and position --- and heard their stories, and their desire to tell their stories openly to the public. And that got my attention very, very rapidly ... the first hand experiences of these credible witnesses that, now, in advanced years are anxious to tell their story. We can't deny that, and the evidence points to the fact that Roswell was a real incident. And that, indeed, an alien craft did crash, and that material was recovered from that crash site."


17. **Storey Musgrove (Space Shuttle)** 
    - Quote: "Statistically it's a certainty there are hugely advanced civilizations, intelligences, life forms out there. I believe they're so advanced they're even doing interstellar travel. I believe it's possible they even came here."

18. **Dr. Brian O'Leary (Apollo Program)** 
    - Quote: "We have contact with alien cultures."

19. **Donald (Deke) Slayton (Mercury)** - 1951 interview
    - Quote: "I was testing a P-51 fighter in Minneapolis when I spotted this object. I was at about 10,000 feet on a nice, bright, sunny afternoon. I thought the object was a kite, then I realized that no kite is gonna fly that high. As I got closer it looked like a weather balloon, gray and about three feet in diameter. But as soon as I got behind the damn thing it didn't look like a balloon anymore. It looked like a saucer, a disk. About the same time, I realized that it was suddenly going way from me --- and there I was, running at about 300 miles per hour. I tracked it for a little way, and then all of a sudden the damn thing just took off. It pulled about a 45 degree climbing turn and accelerated and just flat disappeared."

20. **Major Robert White (X-15 Test Pilot)** - July 17, 1962
    - Quote: "There ARE things out there! There absolutely is!"
    - Note: White exclaiming over the radio about a UFO encounter taking place on a 58 mile high X-15 flight. He later reported: "I have no idea what it could be. It was greyish in color and about thirty to forty feet away."

## Complete Information on User's Initial 8 Astronauts:

### Scott Kelly (Space Shuttle/ISS) - ✓ VERIFIED
- **Role**: NASA UAP study team member (2022)
- **UFO Experience**: F-14 Tomcat incident off Virginia Beach
  - Quote: "I remember one time I was flying in the warning areas off of Virginia Beach military operating area and my RIO — the guy who sits in the back of the Tomcat — was convinced we flew by a UFO. So I didn't see it. We turned around. We went to go look at it. It turns out it was Bart Simpson — a balloon."
- **Space Observations**: Reported seeing unusual behavior in space attributed to atmospheric lensing
  - Quote: "Oftentimes, in space, I would see things and I was like, 'That's really not behaving like it should.' And every single time when I would look at it long enough, I would realize that it was atmospheric lensing."
- **Verification Level**: HIGH - Multiple documented sources, NASA panel member

### Al Worden (Apollo 15) - ✓ VERIFIED  
- **Ancient Aliens Statements**: Good Morning Britain interview (2017)
  - Quote: "We are the aliens, but we just think they are somebody else. But we are the ones who came from somewhere else, because somebody else had to survive, and they got into little spacecraft then they came here and landed, and they started civilisation here. And if you don't believe me, go get books on Ancient Sumerians and see what they had to say. They'll tell you right up front."
- **Mission**: Apollo 15 Command Module Pilot (1971)
- **Time on Moon**: 39 minutes and 7 seconds
- **Verification Level**: HIGH - Video interview evidence, multiple sources

### Jerry Linenger (Mir Space Station) - ✓ VERIFIED
- **Multiple Anomalous Sightings**: 5-month stay on Mir (1997)
  - Quote: "Well I did, I saw things in the true sense of the word, unidentified flying objects – don't take me out of context there – no aliens. But I saw stuff that made me call my crewmates over and say 'what the heck is that?'"
  - Quote: "We'd look and sometimes it was metallic, it was like a spoon if you were off in the distance and in the province of space good luck figuring out if it's a really small object close-by, something floated out of the airlock. Or is it something 100,000 miles away that's the size of a big spacecraft?"
- **Mission Details**: First American to spacewalk from foreign space station, 50 million miles logged, 2,000 Earth orbits
- **Verification Level**: HIGH - Live interview footage, multiple documented sources
