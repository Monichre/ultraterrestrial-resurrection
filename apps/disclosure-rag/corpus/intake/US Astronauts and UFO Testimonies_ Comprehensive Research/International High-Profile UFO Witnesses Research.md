# International High-Profile UFO Witnesses Research

## Initial Key Figures

### 1. Paul Hellyer (Canada)
**Position**: Former Canadian Defense Minister (1960s)
**Background**: Served as Canada's defense minister, cabinet minister during two of Canada's best-known UFO sightings
**Key Claims**:
- First spoke out in 2005 about belief that governments are concealing alien presence
- Claims UFOs are "as prevalent as airplanes in our skies"
- Calls for government disclosure of UFO information
- Spoke at University of Calgary in 2015 demanding truth from governments
- Age 91 at time of 2015 statements

**Authority Level**: Very High - Former Defense Minister of major NATO country
**Source**: https://www.independent.co.uk/news/world/americas/former-canadian-defence-secretary-paul-hellyer-calls-on-governments-to-reveal-ufo-information-10190024.html

### 2. Haim Eshed (Israel)
**Position**: Former head of Israel's Defense Ministry space directorate
**Background**: Respected professor and retired general, oversaw launch of numerous Israeli satellites
**Key Claims** (2020):
- Claims earthlings have been in contact with extraterrestrials from a "galactic federation"
- Says aliens have asked not to publish their presence as "humanity is not ready yet"
- Claims cooperation agreements signed between species
- Alleges "underground base in the depths of Mars" with American astronauts and alien representatives
- Claims President Trump was aware and "on the verge of revealing" information
- Says aliens are seeking to understand "the fabric of the universe"

**Authority Level**: Very High - Former space security chief of major military power
**Source**: https://www.nbcnews.com/news/weird-news/former-israeli-space-security-chief-says-extraterrestrials-exist-trump-knows-n1250333

## Research Notes
- Both figures went public with claims after retirement
- Both held extremely high-level defense/security positions
- Claims made decades after their service
- Both cite government secrecy as reason for delayed disclosure
