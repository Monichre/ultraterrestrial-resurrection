## Detailed Findings on Key American UFO Witnesses

### 1. **Luis Elizondo** - Former Pentagon AATIP Director
**Authority Level**: Very High
**Background**: 
- Former U.S. Army Counterintelligence officer
- Served in Afghanistan, South America, Guantanamo Bay
- Pentagon Office of Under Secretary of Defense for Intelligence (2008-2017)
- Led Advanced Aerospace Threat Identification Program (AATIP) 2009-2017
- University of Miami education (microbiology/immunology)

**UFO Claims**:
- UFOs are of extraterrestrial, cryptid, or interdimensional origin
- U.S. Government is withholding and suppressing UFO information
- Released Pentagon UFO videos to New York Times in 2017
- Resigned in protest of "excessive secrecy and internal opposition"

**Verification Status**: High - Pentagon confirmed his role, though some controversy exists about extent of AATIP involvement

### 2. **David Grusch** - Intelligence Community Whistleblower  
**Authority Level**: Very High
**Background**:
- Former U.S. Air Force intelligence officer
- Afghanistan combat veteran
- National Geospatial-Intelligence Agency (NGA) 
- National Reconnaissance Office (NRO)
- Representative to Unidentified Aerial Phenomena Task Force (2019-2021)
- Helped draft National Defense Authorization Act of 2023

**UFO Claims**:
- U.S. maintains highly secretive UFO retrieval program
- Government possesses multiple "non-human" spacecraft and corpses
- Claims of "white-collar crime" to conceal UFO programs
- People killed to maintain secrecy
- 1933 Italian UFO recovery transferred to U.S. in 1944-45

**Verification Status**: Medium-High - Congressional testimony under oath, but claims based on documents/conversations rather than direct evidence

### 3. **Christopher Mellon** - Former Deputy Assistant Secretary of Defense
**Authority Level**: Very High  
**Background**:
- Deputy Assistant Secretary of Defense for Intelligence (Clinton & Bush administrations)
- U.S. Senate Intelligence Committee staff member
- Colby College (BE), Yale University (MA)
- Worked for senators William Cohen, John Chafee, John Warner, Jay Rockefeller
- Oversight of Department of Defense special access programs

**UFO Claims**:
- Advocate for transparency in government UFO investigations
- Provided Pentagon UFO videos to New York Times
- Co-founded To the Stars Academy with Elizondo
- Believes UFOs represent potential national security threat

**Verification Status**: Very High - Extensive documented government service, credible background

### 4. **David Fravor** - Former Navy Commander
**Authority Level**: High
**Background**:
- U.S. Navy Commander
- F/A-18 Super Hornet pilot
- USS Nimitz Carrier Strike Group
- 18-year military career

**UFO Claims**:
- Witnessed "Tic Tac" UFO during 2004 USS Nimitz incident
- Object exhibited impossible flight characteristics
- Accelerated to "supersonic speeds" instantly
- Congressional testimony under oath (July 2023)

**Verification Status**: Very High - Multiple witnesses, radar confirmation, official Navy acknowledgment

### 5. **Ryan Graves** - Former Navy Pilot
**Authority Level**: High

**Verification Status**: High - Multiple pilot witnesses, radar data, official documentation

### 6. **Tim Gallaudet** - Former Navy Rear Admiral
