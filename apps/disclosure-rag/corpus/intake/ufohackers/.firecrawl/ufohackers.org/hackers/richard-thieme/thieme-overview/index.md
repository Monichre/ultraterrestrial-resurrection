## **Author, Professional Speaker: Richard Thieme**[Direct link to heading](https://www.ufohackers.org/hackers/richard-thieme/thieme-overview\#author-professional-speaker-richard-thieme)

Thieme has given talks on other topics as well, [see a more complete index here](https://infocondb.org/presenter/richard-thieme-neuralcowboy).

Thieme also wrote a monthly column which was compiled in his book [_Islands in the Clickstream_](https://thiemeworks.com/words/islands-in-the-clickstream/). In the original version, no longer in print, Chapter 9 "The Dark Side of the Moon and Beyond" contains columns written between **1997** and **2001** regarding UFOs. With Thieme's permission, I have republished five of these columns [here](https://www.ufohackers.org/hackers/richard-thieme/islands-in-the-clickstream).

Thieme's **2023** fiction book [_Mobius: Out of Time_](https://thiemeworks.com/words/books/mobius-out-of-time/) covers"the deft management of UFO phenomena and research in the intelligence community," among other topics.

> We chase them, and we can't catch them.
