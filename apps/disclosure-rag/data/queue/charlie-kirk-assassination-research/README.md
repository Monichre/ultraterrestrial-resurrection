## Overview

Investigation into the September 10, 2025 assassination of Charlie Kirk at Turning Point USA event in Orem, Utah. Covers the official lone-gunman narrative, the exploding microphone/under-shirt device theory (Jon Bray analysis), Joe Kent's claims about FBI shutdown of investigation, the Texas AES factory angle, and potential Israeli involvement.

## Key Claims

**Official Narrative:**
- Kirk killed by single rifle round from rooftop ~200 yards away
- Suspect Tyler Robinson charged as lone gunman
- FBI treating as domestic political assassination, not foreign nexus

**Exploding Mic Theory:**
- Jon Bray's "Four Pillars" analysis: shirt deformation, decorticate posturing, shrapnel, necklace motion
- Claims blast originated inside shirt at mic area — inconsistent with rifle shot
- Thermal/explosion-mapping models produced
- Video/audio signatures analyzed by independent analysts

**Joe Kent Claims:**
- Alleged FBI told to shutdown investigation
- Foreign involvement thread pursued

**Texas AES Factory Angle:**
- Alleged factory in Texas manufactured microphone components
- Under-shirt device hypothesis involves miniaturized explosive

**Israel Involvement Theory:**
- Alleged Israeli intelligence involvement in planning or cover-up
- No corroboration from mainstream sources

## Hypothesis Set

**H0:** Lone gunman — Tyler Robinson acted alone, rifle shot killed Kirk
**H1:** Concealed device — explosive device in/around mic or under shirt, staged to look like rifle shot
**H2:** Foreign involvement — state actor (Israel or other) involved in planning
**H3:** FBI cover-up — investigation deliberately shut down to protect foreign asset or operation
